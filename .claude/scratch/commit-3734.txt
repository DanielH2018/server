Derive the deployer's setup-role routing from the playbooks

The deployer carried a hand-written map of which playbook and --tags
value apply each setup role, plus a table of roles gated off its host,
and a test re-derived both from the playbooks to catch drift. A wrong
entry is silent: --tags matching nothing exits 0 and the tick records an
apply of nothing (PR #702, #3933).

scripts/deploy_tools/setup_routing.py now reads initial_setup.yml,
k3s-bringup.yml and bootstrap.yml, and evaluates each entry's when:
against the tick host's vars with a strict form of land_reach's gate
evaluator (moved to setup_gates.py). The tick runs it as a subprocess
over origin's tree before the ff-merge. A failed run, or a role whose
entry it cannot read, routes nothing: the role lands in manual_plane for
a hand rather than being applied under a guessed tag.

The off-tick host the printed remediation targets now travels on the
manual_plane ledger line as a `host` key, because monitor-bridge, the
banner and probe.py cannot run the derivation.
